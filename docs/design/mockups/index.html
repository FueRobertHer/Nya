Direction: today and proposed

Home with the same data. Today: the current look with up to five kinds of note under the total and a separate card per fact. Proposed: one Net worth card, a Needs attention list and a This month card, on the ledger tokens (deeper button blue, hairline rows, tabular money, nothing under 12px).

Job: how am I doing, does anything need me?

Home

  • Total and chart in one card; timeline notes open from the chart (Notes, 2) instead of a card of their own.
  • Needs attention appears only when non-empty, and every item opens where it is fixed.
  • The backup failure is a fact for everyone and an instruction only for the admin.

Job: what do I have and owe, and is each number current?

Accounts

  • No Manage mode. The first account is on the first screen; connecting lives behind + Add.
  • Grouped by institution (a failure is per institution), with a one-line summary by type.
  • Account actions in the account sheet; connection actions in the institution's ⋯ menu. Reconnect stays a visible button.

Job: what came in and went out, and is each transaction right?

Activity

  • Opens on the list: one In / Out / Net strip, then search, filters and transactions on the first screen.
  • Analysis moves to Spending, with one period control for every chart (the cash-flow ranges are kept).
  • A transaction opens a sheet instead of an 11-control editor inside the row.

Job: am I on track, what is coming, what am I saving toward?

Budgets

  • Three segments, each with one time range: Month, Upcoming, Goals.
  • Forecast, calendar, recurring and planned items are one view of the same data.
  • One completeness notice per segment instead of a paragraph per card. Debt payoff moves here, under Goals.

Job: where am I, where am I heading, and what changes it? · decided 2026-10-25

Plan

  • The first segment becomes Your path: one answer against your own stop age, one chart from today to that age, then the four rows it rests on.
  • Life events stay in scenarios, and become easy to find: a What if… card on Your path offers templates (a home, a car, travel, a child, a raise, a windfall, a loan that ends). Picking one creates a scenario named after it and shows how it moves your FI age. Your plan stays what you have locked in.
  • Words: "financial independence" and the FI terms stay as labels, plain words carry the meaning ("growth after inflation", "how much you'd draw each year", "you could stop saving now"), and nothing says "retire early".
  • Each input is one row; how it was worked out is in its sheet behind Why?. Caveats stay on the figure: critical ones in full, lesser ones as one line that says how many and what kind.
  • Every result keeps a short "Hypothetical, from US history" label; the full text and data sources are said once, in About these numbers.

Left out of the first version, on purpose. Actual history on the path chart (snapshots keep no daily invested total, and they are in nominal dollars while the plan is in today's), the stress band on the path, event pins, a year-by-year sheet, and an FI line that steps with lasting events. Each is in the ProjectionLab track in the master plan.

Replaces Manage mode

Settings

  • A full screen from the avatar menu (a gear in password mode), with six sections.
  • Everything Manage appended to Accounts lives here, plus theme, sharing and the account itself.
  • A section that needs you shows why on its row, and the avatar carries a dot.

Honest, ranked, never softened

Status: one line, then the full words

A caveat sits on the number it qualifies. Critical caveats always show in full. Two or more caution or information caveats become a summary that says how many and what kind, in the color of the worst one, and expands in place to every note in its current wording.

The three levels. Critical (red): an institution needs reconnecting, rows couldn't be shown, a total is short. Caution (amber): recovered but dated balances, an account that stopped reporting (held at its last known balance), a connection that ends soon, history paused, a backup or clean-up failed, a month may be missing transactions. Information (neutral): converted currency, imported history, accrued interest left out. Anything that asks you to act is also counted in Home's Needs attention.

Nothing moves while it loads

Signing in and loading

  • The sign-in page is laid out from the top: Clerk's form arriving, or changing step, no longer moves the name and the demo card above it.
  • Until the form loads, a placeholder of its size holds its place; the form fades in over it.
  • Under the name, one line on what Nya is. Clerk's Continue is the one filled button: the demo accounts are tonal, each saying what it is set up to hold, above the form; which banks connect is folded under it, behind Which banks work?
  • A demo button signs in on the page, with no page loaded again. From its press, and on Home from the moment a sign-in moves there, the app loading: the dashboard's header and the Net worth card in placeholders, which the dashboard then draws itself while its first load is on its way.
